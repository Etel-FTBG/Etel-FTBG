How The System Works

1️⃣

Registration

Driver arrives at station. Controller enters plate number into system

2️⃣

Token Assigned

System generates unique token and assigns queue position automatically

3️⃣

Driver Checks

Driver uses phone to check position online. Gets notified when close

4️⃣

Service Complete

Controller marks as served. Data logged for government reports
