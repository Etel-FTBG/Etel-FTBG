Who We Are

Etel Group ("Etel Group", "we", "us") is a Hawassa, Ethiopia-based holding company operating ethelmarket.com and its family of subsidiary products, including Etel-ID (identity), Etel Pay (wallet/payments), Etel Market (marketplace), Etel Delivery (delivery & ride), Etel Madeya (fuel-queue management), and others. This policy explains what personal data we collect across these products, why, and what choices you have.

Because several of our products (Etel-ID, Etel Pay) handle identity verification and money, and Etel Madeya is in active use by government-facing pilot programs, we've written this to reflect what our systems actually do today, not aspirational language. If a specific product's own in-app privacy notice is more detailed for that product, the more detailed one governs for that product.

What We Collect

What we collect depends on which product you use:

  • Contact form (ethelmarket.com): name, email address, subject, company (optional), your message, and the IP address the submission came from.
  • Etel-ID (our shared identity layer): full name, phone number, a password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and — where you choose to complete identity verification — the identity details needed for that (e.g. national ID reference), which is verified through Etel-ID rather than re-collected by every subsidiary.
  • Etel Pay: transaction records (sends, top-ups, escrow, disputes) tied to your Etel-ID, needed to operate a wallet at all and to meet basic financial record-keeping obligations.
  • Delivery, Market, Madeya, and other subsidiary products: the order, queue, or listing data specific to that product (e.g. a delivery address, a queue position, a marketplace order) — see that product's own in-app notices for specifics.
  • Automatically, everywhere: basic server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested path, user-agent) generated by normal web server operation, kept only as long as needed for security and abuse investigation.

We do not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on ethelmarket.com. If that changes, we'll update this section and, where required by law, ask for your consent first.

How We Use It

  • To operate the product you're using — logging you in, processing a payment, tracking a delivery, managing a queue position.
  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form.
  • To secure our systems — rate-limiting abusive traffic, investigating suspicious activity, and maintaining audit logs of privileged actions taken by our own staff.
  • To meet legal obligations that apply to us, including basic financial record-keeping for Etel Pay and, where applicable, sharing information with Ethiopian regulators or law enforcement when legally required to do so.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for the full detail — in short, we use a small number of strictly-necessary cookies (mainly session/login tokens for our admin and member-facing tools) and no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies today.

Sharing

Etel-ID exists specifically so that our subsidiaries can share a single identity and a single set of company-role permissions instead of each collecting your data independently — so your Etel-ID profile is visible to the subsidiary products you actually use, scoped to what each one needs. We do not share your personal data with outside third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who process data strictly on our behalf (e.g. our hosting provider), under obligations to protect it.
  • Ethiopian government authorities, where required by law or a valid legal process.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale — this policy would continue to apply to your data under the new ownership.

Security & Data & Security

Passwords are hashed (bcrypt), never stored or logged in plain text. Administrative actions across our systems are increasingly backed by audit logs recording who did what and when — this is an ongoing hardening effort we run as a standing practice, not a one-time project, and we do not consider any system "done" from a security standpoint. Access to production credentials and databases is restricted to the engineers operating each system. No system is perfectly secure, and we treat any credible report of a vulnerability seriously — see Contact below to report one.

Data Retention

We keep personal data for as long as the account or record it relates to is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for legal, financial record-keeping, or dispute-resolution purposes (typically no more than a few years for financial transaction records, per standard practice for payment services). You can ask us to delete data that isn't subject to a legal retention requirement — see Your Rights below.

Your Rights

You can ask us to: tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, delete data we no longer have a legal or operational need to keep, or export a copy of your data. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

Children

Our products are intended for adults capable of entering into agreements (e.g. an Etel Pay wallet or an Etel-ID account) under Ethiopian law, and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever we make a material change to this policy, and where a change significantly affects how we handle your data, we'll make a reasonable effort to give existing account holders more direct notice than just this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request under Your Rights above: info@ethelmarket.com, or use the contact form on our homepage.
