Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies (and similar local-storage mechanisms) are small pieces of data a website can store in your browser. We use them narrowly, and this page explains exactly how.

What We Use Today

  • Strictly-necessary session/authentication tokens. When you log into a member portal or an admin panel across our products (e.g. Etel-ID's member portal, an Etel Group admin session), we use a token — stored as a cookie or in your browser's local storage depending on the product — to keep you signed in between requests. Without this, you'd have to re-authenticate on every single page.
  • Language preference. Where a page offers an English/Amharic toggle, we may remember your choice locally in your browser so you don't have to reselect it every visit.

We do not currently use any advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics) anywhere on ethelmarket.com or its subsidiary products.

Third-Party Cookies

Some subsidiary products embed third-party services for specific features (for example, map rendering for delivery tracking). Where that's the case, the relevant third party may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy, independent of us. We link to those third parties' own policies from within the specific feature where this applies.

Managing Cookies

Since we don't use tracking or advertising cookies, there's nothing to opt out of on that front today. If you'd prefer not to allow the strictly-necessary session cookie, you can block cookies in your browser settings — note that this will prevent you from staying logged into member portals and admin tools, since that's the only thing this cookie does.

Changes to This Policy

If we introduce analytics or any other new category of cookie in the future, we'll update this page first and, where required by law, ask for your consent before setting them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@ethelmarket.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
