🛫 Launch period

While the launch period is on, no payout can be locked: the platform may charge no commission and the raise is spent on growth. One rule, shown everywhere: the launch period ends at most 90 days after it started, or earlier when the 4-week average reaches 150 trips a day. The pitch, the deal sheet and the investor portal all read this rule from here, so change it here only.

💰 Launch budget

What the raise is spent on. Investors see the totals per line and the list of spends. Spending from this budget is not deducted from the profit that is shared. Promotion, bonuses and referral rewards are also counted automatically from Delivery & Ride (from the date set above), so you only record the rest by hand.
