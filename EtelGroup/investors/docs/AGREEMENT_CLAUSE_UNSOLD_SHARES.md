# Draft clause: Unsold Profit Shares

*Draft wording that matches how the payout calculator works today. For review by the Company's lawyer before it goes into the Profit-Share Investment Agreement. Not legal advice.*

## Clause X. Unsold Profit Shares

**X.1 The pool.** The Investor Pool is 2,000 Profit Shares. For each Distribution Period the **Pool Entitlement** is 20% of the Net Profit of that period. If the Net Profit is zero or negative, the Pool Entitlement is zero and nothing is paid under this clause.

**X.2 Unsold Shares.** "Unsold Shares" are the Profit Shares of the Investor Pool that have not been sold and fully paid for by the last day of the Distribution Period.

**X.3 Unsold Share Amount.** For each Distribution Period the **Unsold Share Amount** is: Pool Entitlement x (Unsold Shares / 2,000) x 2.5.

**X.4 Sharing.** One half of the Unsold Share Amount is allocated among the holders of sold Profit Shares in proportion to the number of Profit Shares each holds on the last day of the period (the **Unsold Share Bonus**). The other half is kept by the Company.

**X.5 In addition to the ordinary entitlement.** The Unsold Share Bonus is paid in addition to a holder's ordinary entitlement (including the 2.5x boost during payback). It is paid whether or not the holder has been paid back. It counts as an amount received when working out whether the holder has been paid back.

**X.6 It shrinks and ends.** The Unsold Share Bonus falls as more Profit Shares are sold and ends when all 2,000 Profit Shares are sold. A person who buys Profit Shares in a later Stage takes part from the period in which the purchase is complete and has no claim to an Unsold Share Bonus of an earlier period.

**X.7 Profit only.** The Unsold Share Bonus is paid only out of Net Profit of the period and only when payouts are not paused under the Launch Period clause. The total paid to all holders under this Agreement for a period never exceeds 50% of that period's Net Profit.

**X.8 Disclosure.** The Investor acknowledges having read this clause and the explanation on the Deal Sheet.

### Worked example (for the Deal Sheet or an annex)

Net Profit of the period ETB 100,000; 400 of 2,000 Profit Shares sold.

| Step | ETB |
|---|---|
| Pool Entitlement (20%) | 20,000 |
| Unsold Share Amount: 20,000 x 1,600/2,000 x 2.5 | 40,000 |
| Half shared among holders (Unsold Share Bonus) | 20,000 |
| A holder of 100 shares (100/400 of the holders) receives a bonus of | 5,000 |
| Plus the ordinary boosted entitlement: 20,000 x 100/2,000 x 2.5 | 2,500 |
| **Total for that holder** | **7,500** |
