ETEL Delivery & Ride - Business Model Canvas

Etel Group - Hawassa, Ethiopia

Key Partners

  • Telebirr and banks
  • Restaurants, shops, pharmacies
  • Independent drivers and couriers
  • SMS, push, Telegram providers
  • Hawassa transport authorities
  • INSA (security compliance)
  • Investors

Key Activities

  • Dispatch and ride matching
  • Settlement, escrow, payouts
  • Recruiting drivers and merchants
  • Platform maintenance and audits
  • Marketing and support

Key Resources

  • PHP/MySQL PWA platform
  • EtelID (SSO) and EtelPay wallet
  • Driver and courier network
  • Etel Group brand, local knowledge

Value Propositions

  • Customers: delivery and rides in one app, English and Amharic, Telebirr / bank / EtelPay, live tracking
  • Merchants: ready customers and couriers, inventory dashboard, wallet payouts
  • Drivers: steady income, driver app, wallet settlement
  • All: one EtelID login across every Etel service; installable, offline-capable PWA for low-cost Android

Customer Relationships

  • Self-service app
  • Live order and ride tracking
  • In-app chat and support
  • Personal merchant and driver onboarding

Channels

  • PWA install, ethelmarket.com
  • Push and Telegram
  • Agents and field onboarding
  • Cross-promotion: Gebeya, Taxi, Pay

Customer Segments

  • Hawassa consumers (food, groceries, goods)
  • Ride passengers
  • Merchants (service givers)
  • Couriers and ride drivers
  • Equb / community groups
  • Investors

Cost Structure

  • Driver and courier incentives
  • Payment gateway fees
  • Hosting, domain, SMS, push
  • Staff: support, agents, developers
  • Marketing and acquisition
  • Security audits, refunds and disputes

Revenue Streams

  • 5% commission on merchant orders
  • Zone-based delivery fees
  • Platform cut of ride fares (rate to confirm)
  • EtelPay wallet fees (to confirm)
