## 3. ETEL Drive (`com.etel.driver`) — ride drivers

**Title:** ETEL Drive – Hawassa

**Short description (80):**
Go online, accept ride requests and earn driving with ETEL Ride in Hawassa.

**Full description:**
ETEL Drive is the driver app for ETEL Ride in Hawassa.

Go online when you are ready to work, accept ride requests from nearby passengers, and earn on your own schedule.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Go online or offline whenever you choose
• Receive ride requests near you
• Confirm each trip with the passenger's trip PIN
• See busy areas where riders are asking for trips
• Print receipts on a Bluetooth printer (optional)
• Works on basic phones and slow connections

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your ETEL driver account
2. Tap Go Online
3. Accept a request and drive to the pickup
4. Enter the passenger's PIN to start the trip
5. Complete the trip

REQUIREMENTS
A driver account approved by ETEL, a valid licence and a registered vehicle. Location access is needed while you are online so passengers can be matched with you.

Want to ride instead? Download ETEL Ride.

Support: [support email / phone]

## Data Safety (declare accurately)

- **Location (precise, approximate):** collected by Ride, Drive and Courier, for core functionality. Delivery uses delivery address.
- **Personal info:** name, phone number, email, address.
- **Financial info:** payment info is handled by Chapa; declare if payment details pass through the app.
- **Photos:** drivers and couriers if ID photos are uploaded.
- **Data encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS). Provide a way to request account deletion; Play requires it.
