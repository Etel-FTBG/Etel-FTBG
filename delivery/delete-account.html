Delete Your ETEL Account
Applies to ETEL Delivery, ETEL Ride, ETEL Drive, ETEL Courier and ETEL Service
How to request deletion

In the app: open your Profile or Settings menu and tap Delete my account, then confirm. This sends your request to the ETEL team.

Without the app: email privacy@ethelmarket.com with the subject Account deletion request. Include the app name and the phone number you registered with, so we can find the account.

We will contact you within 5 business days to confirm the request is really from you, then delete the account.

What we delete
  • Your profile: name, phone number, and sign-in details
  • Saved addresses, favourite routes, and app settings
  • Push notification subscriptions on your devices
  • For drivers and couriers: the selfie, licence photo and vehicle details you uploaded
What we may keep
  • Order, trip and payment records, kept only as long as the law, tax rules or an open dispute requires. Where possible these are separated from your name and phone number.
  • Records needed to investigate fraud or safety incidents.

Once the legal or dispute reason ends, those records are deleted too. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.

Before you delete
  • Deletion is permanent. You cannot sign in to that account again.
  • Spend or withdraw any ETEL Pay wallet balance first. Contact us if you need help.
  • Drivers, couriers and shops: finish any active trips, deliveries or open orders first.
