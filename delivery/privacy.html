1. Who We Are

ETEL Group (ኢትኤል ቡድን) operates ETEL Delivery, ETEL Ride, ETEL Pay, and related services from Hawassa, Sidama Region, Ethiopia. When you use any ETEL product, you are sharing information with us as described in this policy.

ኢትኤል ቡድን በሀዋሳ ከተማ ሲዳማ ክልል ኢትዮጵያ ውስጥ ETEL ዴሊቨሪ፣ ETEL ራይድ፣ ETEL ፔይ እና ተዛማጅ አገልግሎቶችን ያሰሩ ድርጅት ነው።

2. Information We Collect

Account information: Your name, phone number, and password when you register.

Location data: GPS coordinates while using ETEL Ride or Delivery so we can match you with drivers or couriers and route your order. Drivers' locations are shared with passengers during active rides only.

Trip and order data: Pickup/drop-off addresses, fare amounts, timestamps, and payment method for each transaction.

Payment data: ETEL Pay wallet balance and transaction history. We do not store card numbers — all card processing is handled by Telebirr or CBE Birr.

Chat messages: In-ride messages between drivers and passengers. These are stored only for the duration of the active ride and permanently deleted within 24 hours of completion.

Device data: Browser type and approximate network location for security and fraud prevention.

እኛ የምንሰበስበው መረጃ፡ ስም፣ ስልክ ቁጥር፣ ቦታ (GPS)፣ ጉዞ እና ትዕዛዝ ታሪክ፣ እና የክፍያ ታሪክ ነው።

3. How We Use Your Information
  • Matching passengers with nearby drivers and routing rides
  • Processing payments and maintaining wallet balances
  • Sending OTP verification codes via Telegram or SMS
  • Calculating and displaying earnings for drivers and partners
  • Resolving disputes and investigating fraud
  • Improving our services through anonymised analytics
  • Sending safety SOS alerts to our admin team when triggered

We never sell your personal data to third parties or use it for advertising.

የእርስዎን ግላዊ ውሂብ ለሶስተኛ ወገን አንሸጥም ወይም ለማስታወቂያ አንጠቀምም።

4. Location Data

ETEL Ride and ETEL Delivery require location access to function. Location tracking is active only while the app is open and during an active ride or delivery. We stop collecting location data the moment you close the app or the ride/delivery completes.

Your precise location is shared with your assigned driver or courier during the active service window only, and is not shared with other users or stored beyond 30 days.

የቦታ ትሮኪን ወቅት አፕሊኬሽኑ ክፍት ሆኖ ሲሆን ብቻ ነው። ሌሎች ተጠቃሚዎች የእርስዎን ትክክለኛ ቦታ ማየት አይችሉም።

5. Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. Trip and order history is retained for 2 years to support dispute resolution. Chat messages are deleted within 24 hours of ride completion. SOS alerts are retained for 90 days. You may request full deletion of your account at any time.

6. Your Rights
  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your name or phone number through the Profile screen
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your trip and order history in CSV format

እርስዎ ማድረግ የሚችሉ ነገሮች፡ ውሂብዎን ማዳረስ፣ ማስተካከል፣ ወይም ሙሉ በሙሉ ማሰረዝ።

7. Security

Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing. All API calls require a signed bearer token. Sensitive configuration (database credentials, API keys) is stored in environment files excluded from backups. HTTPS is enforced on all endpoints. We conduct regular security audits of our codebase.

8. Children

ETEL services are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be communicated via the app. Continued use of ETEL services after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
