# Etel brand - master logo (source of truth)

`public/images/etel-master-logo.webp` (1030x1024) and `etel-master-logo-512.webp`
are the **Etel Family Technology and Business Group - Hawassa 2026** master
coin supplied by the owner. Every redesign decision in EtelID (login, portal,
ID card, NFC pages, print, emails) must start from this mark.

What the mark is made of (keep all of it recognisable):

* Round coin with a heavy antique-gold / bronze relief rim and a ring of
  green-yellow-red beads (the Ethiopian colours).
* Deep forest-green field carrying the text "Etel Family Technology and
  business Group" and the Amharic line "ኢትኤል ቤተሰብ ተከኖሎጂ እና ቢዝነስ ግሩፕ".
* Centre medallion: the blue-and-gold **ኢ / E** mark with two arrows, "ETEL"
  and "ኢትኤል" below it, framed by ornate gold scrollwork.
* Two ribbons ("ኢት-ዮጵ" / "hawassa"), a blue disc with the eight-pointed
  star and "2026", and the words "ኢትኤል" and "ሀዋሳ".
